P.06

Acrylic
Chimes

A site-specific wind installation suspended beneath a pergola, composed of laser-cut acrylic panels hung on a tensioned steel cable system. The panels catch and diffuse sunlight as they move, casting shifting light patterns across the ground and surrounding walls. The project explores ephemerality and environmental responsiveness — the installation is never the same twice, animated entirely by wind and light.
