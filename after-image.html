After
Image

Located in the main quad at Woodbury University in Burbank, this project introduces a student lounge within one of the campus's most active circulation zones. Positioned along a primary pedestrian path, it creates a transitional space for gathering, pause, and informal performance. Using footage from Into the Spider-Verse, moments of movement were tracked and abstracted into geometric traces, then extruded and iteratively assembled into a spatial system. Influenced by the film's glitch sequences, the final form consolidates layered motion into a faceted geometry that reads as a frozen moment of temporal distortion.
